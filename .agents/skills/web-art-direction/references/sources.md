# Sources and synthesis

The skill is an independently worded synthesis of the following public skill instructions, reviewed November 26, 2026. It is not a bundle of their code or a reproduction of their rule files.

- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), Paul Bakaus (Apache-2.0): context-specific surface modes, deliberate design direction, separation of design critique and technical checks, and verification against actual rendering. The upstream detector is optional and not bundled. [Reviewed entrypoint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/.agent/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md).
- [Unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop), Damir Sokolovsky (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): content-derived character, systematic inspection of visual layers, editing existing interfaces while preserving purpose. Adapted into flexible categories rather than a mandatory sequence, color scheme, or fixed type weights. [Reviewed entrypoint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/heydamir/unslop/main/skills/unslop/SKILL.md).
- [Taste Skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill), also published under [tasteskill/tasteskill](https://github.com/tasteskill/tasteskill/blob/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md) and [jeettrench/taste-skill](https://github.com/jeettrench/taste-skill): broad layout exploration, art direction, purposeful assets, avoidance of repetitive visual defaults, and actual-browser checks. Excluded mandatory marketing structures, numerical design knobs, prescriptive stacks, fixed font lists, and animation requirements. The originally linked repository root returned 404 during review; indexed skill text and the alternate repository were available.
- [Frontend Craft](https://github.com/nattergabriel/frontend-craft): context-dependent design judgment, recognizing that tells change over time, preserving existing design systems, fixing repeated issues at their source, and rechecking regressions. [Reviewed entrypoint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nattergabriel/frontend-craft/main/skills/frontend-craft/SKILL.md).
- [Anti-Slop Design](https://github.com/nyrthoughts/anti-slop-design) (MIT): organized smell checklist, before/after audit, reasoned exceptions, and multi-width verification. Excluded universal font, palette, spacing, and animation mandates. [Reviewed entrypoint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nyrthoughts/anti-slop-design/main/skills/anti-slop-ui/SKILL.md).

Additional editorial guidance comes from the user's request for non-generic minimal design and copy, and the no-cringe-writing skill's emphasis on authentic voice, concrete meaning, and editing by subtraction. The master skill extends these principles to all visible website copy and expressly protects useful functional language and approved expressive writing.

The synthesis intentionally favors contextual judgment over absolute aesthetic bans, is framework-independent, and requires a review step but no extra runtime or lint dependency. It does not claim to reproduce every upstream detector or playbook. No upstream code is bundled.
